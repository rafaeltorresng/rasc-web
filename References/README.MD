# Design References

References inform execution quality; they are not a source of truth for Rasc identity. Keep the approved hierarchy below. Sources are official homepages and should be rechecked before a future design pass.

| Reference | Role | Borrow | Avoid | Relevant Rasc sections | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Apple (primary) | Native-product trust and restrained product storytelling. | Product framing, whitespace, legibility, platform context. | Copying Apple’s visual identity, UI, icons, or claims. | Hero, Capture → Refine → Keep, trust/compatibility. | https://www.apple.com/macos/ |
| ElevenLabs (primary) | Clear high-quality product demonstration. | How a real product demo supports a concise narrative. | Dark AI category cues, glowing effects, or its brand system. | Hero demo, Refine proof. | https://elevenlabs.io/ |
| Cursor (primary) | Direct product-led communication for a Mac utility audience. | Clear hierarchy and product proof. | Devtool positioning, dark/neon language, or borrowed UI. | Hero, interaction story. | https://www.cursor.com/ |
| Intercom (primary) | Editorial clarity and restrained explanation of a product workflow. | Section pacing, useful detail, readable copy. | SaaS feature-grid patterns or AI-first category cues. | Narrative sections, trust. | https://www.intercom.com/ |
| Vercel (secondary) | Technical craft and polished delivery. | Precise layout and small details. | Dark-first gradients and developer-platform identity. | Responsive execution, motion restraint. | https://vercel.com/ |
| Superhuman (secondary) | Premium keyboard-first utility communication. | Shortcut clarity, focus, concise product explanation. | Luxury styling or copied product motifs. | Capture, shortcuts, interaction framing. | https://superhuman.com/ |
| Raycast (anti-reference) | Category contrast. | Nothing to imitate; use only to identify an overly dark/neon devtool direction. | Dark/neon AI/devtool aesthetic and product-category drift. | Entire landing, especially hero and trust. | https://www.raycast.com/ |
| Linear (anti-reference) | Category contrast. | Nothing to imitate; use only to spot excessive dark product chrome. | Dark/neon devtool aesthetic, copied gradients, or UI motifs. | Entire landing. | https://linear.app/ |
| Resend (anti-reference) | Category contrast. | Nothing to imitate; use only to flag generic AI/devtool visual cues. | Dark/neon aesthetic and copied visual components. | Entire landing. | https://resend.com/ |

Never copy brand colors, typography, logos, proprietary graphics, or page sections wholesale. External references cannot override Rasc’s approved positioning, landing narrative/IA, Brand Identity v1 direction, or real app assets.
